Jaigarh Fort

The invincible fort of the world's largest cannon — Jaipur, Rajasthan

About this site

Jaigarh is the stern, purely military twin of the pleasure-palace Amber below it — three kilometres of massive ramparts on the Cheel ka Teela hill, built for war, not court life.

Its pride is the Jaivana, the largest cannon on wheels ever made: a 50-tonne, 20-foot barrel cast in the fort's own foundry in 1720 and test-fired only once. The fort also holds vast granaries, a cannon foundry, temples and sweeping views over Amber and Maota Lake.

History

Sawai Jai Singh II built Jaigarh in 1726 to guard Amber from above, linking the two forts into one defensive system. Its foundries cast the artillery that armed the Kachhwaha armies.

The fort was never captured in battle. In the 1970s it made headlines when the government searched it for a legendary hidden treasure of the Jaipur royals — nothing was officially found.

With Amber and Nahargarh, Jaigarh completes Jaipur's great triangle of hill forts, all visible from one another across the ridge.
