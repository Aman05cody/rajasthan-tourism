About Rajasthan

Rajasthan, India's largest state, was once a land of proud Rajput kingdoms — Mewar, Marwar, Amber, Bikaner and Jaisalmer. Their legacy survives in some of the finest forts and palaces on earth, six of which are UNESCO World Heritage Sites under the "Hill Forts of Rajasthan" listing.

This website is your guide to twelve of the greatest heritage sites of Rajasthan. Visit the Heritage page to browse them all, open any site for its story and history, wander through the Gallery, and plan your stay on the Hotel Booking page.

Plan Your Visit

Best time to visit

October to March, when the desert heat eases and festivals like the Pushkar Fair and Desert Festival light up the state.

Getting around

Jaipur, Jodhpur and Udaipur are well connected by rail and air. The classic route — Delhi → Jaipur → Jodhpur → Jaisalmer → Udaipur — covers most sites on this website.
